Redefining Modern Elegance

Founded in 2010 by visionary designer Christian Wijaya, our brand emerged from a simple yet powerful idea: true luxury doesn't shout, it whispers. What began as a small atelier in Jakarta has grown into an internationally recognized label, yet we've never lost sight of our artisanal roots.

Each Christian Wijaya piece is conceived as a future heirloom - designed to transcend seasons and trends. Our collections blend impeccable tailoring with innovative fabrications, resulting in wearable art that stands the test of time.

We believe in fashion that respects both the wearer and the maker. That's why we maintain direct relationships with our workshops and source materials with uncompromising standards for quality and sustainability.

Our Philosophy

Three pillars guide everything we create at Christian Wijaya

Timeless Design

We create pieces meant to be worn for years, not just seasons. Our designs balance contemporary relevance with enduring appeal, ensuring your investment lasts.

Artisanal Craft

Conscious Luxury

True luxury respects its origins. We're committed to ethical sourcing, responsible production, and creating pieces meant to be cherished.
